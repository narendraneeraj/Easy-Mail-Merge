<h3>2.2 Gmail API Access</h3>
<p>Easy Mail Merge uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization process to access Gmail functionality with your permission.</p>
<p>We request the Gmail API permission:</p>
<p><code>https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send</code></p>
<p>This permission allows Easy Mail Merge to send emails through your authorized Gmail account.</p>
<p>Easy Mail Merge does not require your Gmail password. The Gmail sending permission does not authorize the application to read your entire Gmail inbox.</p>
<p>We use Gmail API access only to perform email-sending functions requested by you.</p>
<h3>2.3 Contact Lists and Email Content</h3>
<p>You may upload CSV or Excel files containing recipient email addresses, names, and other personalization fields.</p>
<p>Easy Mail Merge processes this information to prepare and send personalized messages.</p>
<p>In the current serverless version, recipient lists and email content are processed in your browser or extension. They are not intentionally uploaded to our Supabase user database.</p>
<p>Some campaign information may be stored locally in your browser to support drafts, progress tracking, or campaign history.</p>
<p>Email content and recipient information are transmitted to Google through the Gmail API when you initiate sending.</p>
