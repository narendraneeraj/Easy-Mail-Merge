The Beauty of Simplicity

Good design is as little design as possible. Less, but better — because it concentrates on the essential aspects, and the products are not burdened with non-essentials. Back to purity, back to simplicity.

This tiny template uses Bootstrap 6's latest grid and utility classes to keep everything compact and flexible. The header and footer stay consistent, while the body remains open for content. It’s a perfect starting point for a clean, no-clutter website.
