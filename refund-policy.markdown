<h2>1. Overview</h2>

<p>
  At Easy Mail Merge, we aim to provide a reliable and user-friendly
  Gmail mail merge service.
</p>

<p>
  This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how subscription
  cancellations, payment-related issues, and refund requests
  will be handled.
</p>

<h2>2. Free Plan</h2>

<p>
  Easy Mail Merge may offer a free plan with limited features
  and email sending allowances.
</p>

<p>
  Since no subscription payment is required for the free plan,
  no refund applies to its use.
</p>

<h2>3. Paid Subscription Plans</h2>

<p>
  When paid plans become available, Easy Mail Merge may offer
  monthly, annual, or other subscription options.
</p>

<p>
  The applicable price, billing period, renewal terms, and
  included features will be displayed before purchase.
</p>

<p>
  Users should review these details before confirming payment.
</p>

<h2>4. Subscription Cancellation</h2>

<p>
  Users will be able to request cancellation of an active paid
  subscription by contacting our support team or using an
  account cancellation feature if one is provided.
</p>

<p>
  Unless otherwise specified at checkout, cancellation will
  prevent future renewals but will not automatically result
  in a refund for the current billing period.
</p>

<p>
  Access to paid features may continue until the end of the
  current paid period, subject to the applicable subscription terms.
</p>

<h2>5. Refund Eligibility</h2>

<p>
  Refund requests may be considered in circumstances such as:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Duplicate charges for the same subscription.</li>
  <li>Incorrect billing amounts.</li>
  <li>Unauthorized transactions, subject to verification.</li>
  <li>Significant technical problems preventing use of paid features.</li>
  <li>Other circumstances where a refund is required by applicable law.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  Refund decisions will depend on the circumstances,
  payment provider rules, applicable subscription terms,
  and relevant consumer protection laws.
</p>

<h2>6. Non-Refundable Situations</h2>

<p>
  Subject to applicable law, refunds may not be available
  in situations including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Unused email sending allowances during an active billing period.</li>
  <li>Changes of mind after purchasing a subscription.</li>
  <li>Failure to use available subscription features.</li>
  <li>Gmail account restrictions or sending limits imposed by Google.</li>
  <li>Email delivery failures caused by third-party email systems.</li>
  <li>Suspension due to violations of our Terms of Service.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies
  that cannot legally be excluded.
</p>

<h2>7. Gmail Sending Limits</h2>

<p>
  Easy Mail Merge sends emails through the Gmail API,
  subject to Google's applicable sending limits,
  restrictions, and account policies.
</p>

<p>
  Purchasing a subscription will not increase or override
  Google's sending limits.
</p>

<p>
  Subscription email allowances represent limits within
  Easy Mail Merge, not guaranteed delivery quantities.
</p>

<h2>8. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments</h2>

<p>
  If you believe you have been charged more than once
  or charged an incorrect amount, please contact us with
  your transaction reference and payment date.
</p>

<p>
  We will review the issue and coordinate with the
  payment provider as necessary.
</p>

<h2>9. How to Request a Refund</h2>

<p>
  To request a refund after paid subscriptions are launched,
  contact our support team at
  <a href="mailto:care@easycalculator.org">care@easycalculator.org</a>.
</p>

<p>Please provide:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Your registered Easy Mail Merge email address.</li>
  <li>The subscription plan purchased.</li>
  <li>The transaction or payment reference number.</li>
  <li>The date of payment.</li>
  <li>A brief explanation of the refund request.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  Please do not send passwords, full payment card numbers,
  or other sensitive authentication information.
</p>

<h2>10. Refund Processing</h2>

<p>
  Approved refunds will generally be returned through the
  original payment method where supported.
</p>

<p>
  Processing times may vary depending on the payment
  provider, bank, and payment method.
</p>

<p>
  Any legally required refund timelines will be followed.
</p>

<h2>11. Changes to This Policy</h2>

<p>
  We may update this Refund and Cancellation Policy
  to reflect changes in subscription plans, payment
  methods, legal requirements, or business operations.
</p>

<p>
  Updated terms will be published on this page
  with a revised Last Updated date.
</p>
