        recipient information from spreadsheets with personalized
        email messages.
      </p>
      <p>
        Instead of manually writing and sending the same email to

        personalize it using fields such as first name, company,
        or other spreadsheet columns.
      </p>
      <p>
        Easy Mail Merge uses Google's OAuth authorization process
        and Gmail API to send messages through the user's
        connected Google account.

    <h2>Privacy and Security Matter</h2>

    <p>
      Easy Mail Merge uses Google OAuth 2.0 authorization to connect
      with Gmail. Users grant the permissions required for the
      email-sending functionality they choose to use.
    </p>

    <p>
      We do not ask users to share their Gmail passwords.
      Our account system is designed to store limited profile
      information, such as name, email address, account creation
      date, and email sending count.
    </p>

    <p>
      Users can revoke the application's Google account access
      through their Google account settings.
    </p>
